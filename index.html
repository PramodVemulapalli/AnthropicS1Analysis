What makes up the $895.4B?

The annual payment table is just one component.

    S-1/A, pp. 174–175. Components sum to $895.393B; the $0.007B difference is rounding. Categories shown as reported, not by vendor.

    Three different kinds of certainty

    $320.0B has a payment schedule. The table includes fixed and determinable obligations at September 30, 2026. Delivery changes can still move the timing or amounts.

    $575.4B sits outside that table. This consists of not-yet-commenced leases, short-term agreements and subsequent commitments. No complete annual schedule is provided for these categories.

    A further $150–175B is estimated. Arrangements without a fixed dollar commitment are excluded from the $895.4B aggregate. These estimates are not another fixed contractual minimum.

    S-1/A, pp. 174–176. $575.4B is derived from the rounded headline less $319.993B.

    Read the time buckets carefully. The $69.8B next-12-month amount overlaps the $447.5B near-term amount; do not add them. The $213.8B conditional subset is also already included in $895.4B, and cannot be treated as an assured reduction.

    04 / Original → Amendment

    What actually changed?

    The financial reporting date moves from June 30 to September 30, 2026. A larger balance is not necessarily the gross value of new contracts: payments, amendments and the measurement date can all affect it.

    Purchase commitments+$48.2B

    Note 13 reports $265.6B at June 30 in the original and $313.8B at September 30 in the amendment: an 18.1% increase in the disclosed remaining fixed/minimum commitments. These are purchase commitments, not the full compute portfolio. Original p. F-59; amendment p. F-60.

    Google hosting minimum+$6.8B

    $114.5B → $121.3B (+5.9%). The agreement’s disclosed amendment period extends from May–August to May–October 2026. The April 2026–July 2033 window is unchanged. Original p. 171; amendment p. 175.

    Microsoft end date+1 month

    The spending period ends in May 2033 in the original and June 2033 in the amendment. The disclosed minimum remains $44.9B. Original p. 171; amendment p. 175.

    New aggregate visibility$895.4B

    The original MD&A aggregate, annual table and key reconciliation amounts are blank. The amendment fills these in and identifies $213.8B with specified termination rights or pre-commencement conditions. An increase in the overall aggregate cannot be calculated from those original blanks. Original pp. 170–171; amendment pp. 174–175.

    More explicit conditionalityClarified terms

    The amendment describes additional TPU delivery/financing alternatives and AMD conditions that may be met or waived by Anthropic. The $125–150B TPU estimate and >$20B initial AMD expenditure expectation remain. The amendment gives a broader excluded-arrangements estimate of $150–175B; the comparable overall range is blank in the original MD&A. These are different scopes, so the TPU range is not a before/after comparator for the broader range.

    Other named amountsUnchanged

    Amazon $110.0B; SpaceXAI up to $120.5B; Nscale up to approximately $44.6B; Lambda approximately $41.3B; Broadcom rent approximately $161.2B excluding interest. The amendment adds that Broadcom’s October assignment did not increase aggregate rent. Original pp. 172–173; amendment pp. 176–177.
